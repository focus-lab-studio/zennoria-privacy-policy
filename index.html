Information Collection and Use

The app may collect certain information to provide core functionality, including:

    Health-related values entered by users are stored solely to provide wellness tracking features. These values are not reviewed by medical professionals and should not be used as a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

  • Device information (device type, operating system version)

Anonymous Account

Zennoria may automatically create an anonymous account using Supabase authentication to enable app functionality. This process does not require users to provide personal information such as name, email, or phone number.

AI Features

Zennoria includes AI-powered features such as chatbot guidance, wellness suggestions, and personalized content. User messages entered into the chatbot may be securely processed through external AI services to generate relevant responses.

Third-Party Services

The app uses third-party services that may collect information used to provide app functionality:

  • Google Play Services
  • Google AdMob (for advertisements)
  • Google Play Billing (for in-app purchases and subscriptions)
  • Supabase (for anonymous authentication and data storage)

These services may collect technical information such as device identifiers, IP address, and usage data in accordance with their respective privacy policies.

Advertisements

Zennoria uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may use advertising identifiers and device information to provide relevant ads.

Payments and Subscriptions

All payments for premium features and subscriptions are securely processed by Google Play Billing. Zennoria does not collect, process, or store payment card details.

Google Play may provide the app with subscription status information (such as whether a user has an active premium subscription) so that premium features can be enabled or disabled appropriately.

Data Security

We value your trust and strive to use commercially acceptable means of protecting your data. Data transmitted between the app and external services is encrypted in transit using secure protocols (HTTPS).

Delete Account and Data

Zennoria allows users to delete their data at any time directly within the app.

Steps to delete data:

  1. Open the Zennoria app
  2. Go to Settings
  3. Select Reset
  4. Confirm the action

When data is deleted, the following information is removed:

  • Height and weight information
  • Health-related values such as blood pressure and sugar data
  • App preferences stored locally on the device
  • Chat data associated with the anonymous account

Anonymous authentication IDs created automatically by Supabase may be retained temporarily for technical purposes, but they are not linked to personally identifiable information.
